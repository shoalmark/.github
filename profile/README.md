*⌜shoalmark⌟ runs today; ⌜skills⌟ is ready to use and ⌜hail/1⌟ to read; ⌜chart/1⌟ and ⌜tracker/1⌟ are planned. Take up
one or more; the final word stays yours.*

Building blocks for builders who run agents. Each stands alone and composes, when you choose, through plain files in
git. In each, the agents do the work and the person in charge has the final word.

- **[shoalmark](https://github.com/shoalmark/shoalmark):** ⌜shoalmark⌟, a workbench for your agents, inside your
  repository: work items and your decisions in Markdown, and a board that puts what waits for you first. Its tracker is
  planned as a block of its own, ⌜tracker/1⌟.
- **[hail](https://github.com/shoalmark/hail):** ⌜hail/1⌟, the protocol by which agent seats hail each other as ships
  do, short and on the record. First edition, not yet in force; tools next.
- **[skills](https://github.com/shoalmark/skills):** ⌜skills⌟, procedures by which agent seats keep their course in
  any git repository, finding drift and pruning stale prose.
